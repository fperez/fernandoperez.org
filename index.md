# Welcome

I am a Professor in the [Department of Statistics](http://statistics.berkeley.edu) at UC Berkeley, and a Faculty Scientist at the [Scientific Data Division](https://scidata.lbl.gov) of Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. I am the faculty director for the Berkeley Institute for Data Science ([BIDS](http://bids.berkeley.edu)), and co-director for the Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center for Data Science & Environment ([DSE](https://dse.berkeley.edu/)). I co-founded, and continue to advise, the [International Interactive Computing Collaboration - 2i2c.org](https://2i2c.org).  I also co-founded [Archean](https://archean.com), a company that uses AI to accelerate the design, synthesis and manufacture cycle for industrially impactful materials, to solve some of our planet's most pressing problems.

My research interests are shaped by years of working on applied problems in physics, mathematics, neuroscience, earth science and health care. I focus on building open tools, systems and infrastructure to help ensure that AI serves humanity and life on our planet, by helping us improve the quality of our science and education. To this end, I focus on applications in [biomedicine](https://bcbi.berkeley.edu) and [health](https://jupyterhealth.org) (ranging from basic science through clinical care), and collaborate with earth and environmental scientists.

While the arrival of AI is transforming all of computing, science and education, I remain convinced of the importance for humans to both understand and drive the decisions we make, as we will ultimately bear their consequences. That means having access to open scientific tools that we can all share, understand and evolve according to our needs and criteria, so we can build knowlege as a common good of humanity. I created the [IPython](http://ipython.org) project while a graduate student in 2001, which has now evolved into the large and collaborative [Project Jupyter](https://jupyter.org), where a talented team does all the hard work.  Today, I continue to work on how these open ecosystems should evolve in order to best serve our human needs while leveraging AI as an oppoortunity.

:::{note} Photo note: the mountains
:class: dropdown

The picture at the top is Colorado's incredible Continental Divide, specifically the Indian Peaks range, shot from [Brainard Lake](http://maps.google.com/maps/place?cid=1317471477502605538&q=brainard+lake,+colorado&hl=en) during a [beautiful fall day in 2006](https://photos.app.goo.gl/a3T1FoFUuaskh41JA). On the descent from that hike I spotted a great-looking [little couloir](https://photos.app.goo.gl/NQQKFGVR3QklzPZ52) that provided for a very nice [alpine day](https://photos.app.goo.gl/NQQKFGVR3QklzPZ52) one week later. Having that terrain 45 minutes from home is one thing (among many) that makes the Colorado Front Range the most amazing place to live I've known.
